
##  Financial Literacy Gap Analysis

__Overview__
Designed and distributed a structured survey instrument across NUCES 
students to collect behavioral data on personal finance habits. Gathered 
63 responses spanning demographics including age, gender, degree program 
and year of study.

Processed and visualized response patterns through Excel, identifying 
trends across budgeting behavior, monthly savings rates, credit 
understanding and expense tracking. Conducted quantitative analysis to 
measure the gap between financial awareness and actual financial behavior 
— a core problem in fintech and policy design.


## Key Findings
- 81% never received any formal financial literacy training
- 35% save nothing monthly despite 84% believing Financial Literacy is important
- 24% do not understand credit cards or interest rates
- Composite Literacy Index: 61.7% across all respondents
